The idea

Your phone already has the actions. GestureFlow makes them reachable.

Most shortcuts stop at launching an app. GestureFlow is built for what happens after that: choosing, searching, calling, navigating, timing, testing, and chaining.

Privacy and control

You decide which powers GestureFlow gets.

GestureFlow is designed around gestures you create and actions you trigger. Permissions are explained in context and tied to the features you choose.

The app explains why access matters before sensitive capabilities such as overlay, notifications, calling, camera, flashlight, or alarms.
1
Permissions are feature-based.

Overlay, notifications, contacts, phone, camera, and alarms are requested only when they support a chosen gesture or access mode.

2
You can stop live access.

Bubble and notification mode are user controlled, visible, and can be stopped from inside the app.

3
Reliability stays transparent.

Health checks show what contributes to dependable access and what needs setup.

4
Public policy ready.

The privacy policy is available at universityhub.co.in/gestureflow/privacy-policy.
