Who we are

University Hub is a student app and website for syllabus, study materials, previous papers, AI study help, notifications, community posts, jobs, resume building, GPA tracking, timetable planning, and attendance support. It is offered by Mervin Technologies and published on Google Play as University Hub.

The same Android package, com.ku.engineering, was first released on January 30, 2023 under the name Kakatiya University (KU) B.Tech, with an earlier privacy policy at engineeringvlog.blogspot.com. That name and that policy no longer describe the service. Use this page as the current Privacy Policy.

Privacy contact: admin@universityhub.co.in

What this policy covers

  • The University Hub Android app.
  • The public website at universityhub.co.in, including account, community, academy, and resume-maker pages.
  • Backend services we use to run those products, including Google Firebase.

GestureFlow and DualLock, if linked from this website, have their own privacy policies. This page does not replace those policies.

Data we collect

Depending on how you use University Hub, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account data: name, email address, sign-in credentials, Google Sign-In account details, and authentication identifiers when you create an account or sign in on the app or website.
  • Profile and academic data: university, college, course, branch, semester, regulation, grading setup, profile image, biography, social links, and related profile details you choose to provide.
  • User-generated content: uploaded files, notes, PDFs, images, comments, posts, discussions, answers, messages, study content, task contributions, and other materials you submit.
  • Student tool data: resume details, GPA entries, attendance records, timetable entries, and similar information you enter into those tools. Some of this stays on your device or in your browser. Information tied to a signed-in feature may be stored with your account.
  • AI feature inputs and outputs: text, files, extracted text, prompts, and generated responses when you use the AI tutor, topic lessons, summaries, question extraction, or similar study assistance.
  • Notification data: push notification tokens, topic subscriptions, local reminder settings, and notification preferences.
  • Device and usage data: app interactions, approximate technical device information, advertising identifiers, network details, and similar operational metadata collected by us or our service providers, including Google Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, and Meta Audience Network.
  • Locally stored data: cached study content, downloaded files, saved preferences, reminders, favorites, notes, a local database on the device, and academy progress stored in your browser.

The app does not request your location or your contacts. Camera access is used only when you start a feature that needs it, such as capturing a study image, a profile image, or a scan.

How we use data

  • To create and manage accounts and let you sign in with email and password, Google Sign-In, or a supported guest flow.
  • To match syllabus, materials, and study flow to your university, college, branch, semester, regulation, and related profile settings.
  • To host, sync, display, and manage uploaded materials, discussions, comments, posts, jobs, resumes, and other content.
  • To run student tools such as GPA tracking, attendance support, timetable planning, and resume building.
  • To process files and text for study features such as text extraction, AI summaries, question extraction, the AI tutor, and topic lessons.
  • To send push notifications, topic updates, local reminders, and app-related operational messages.
  • To provide community, leaderboard, and contribution features on the app and website.
  • To display ads through Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network and support monetization of the app.
  • To maintain security, detect abuse, troubleshoot issues, understand how the service is used, and comply with legal obligations.

How data may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share data in the following situations:

  • With service providers: Google Firebase for authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions, push messaging, in-app messaging, analytics, app integrity checks, and related infrastructure. Google Play services may also process information needed to install, update, and run the app.
  • With advertising providers: Google AdMob and related Google advertising services, and Meta Audience Network, used to deliver ads in the app.
  • With AI providers: Google AI or Gemini-related services when you choose an AI-powered feature. The text, files, and prompts needed for that request are sent so the feature can return an answer.
  • With other users: when you intentionally upload materials, create posts, participate in discussion features, publish profile information, or otherwise share content in community areas.
  • For legal or safety reasons: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our terms, protect users, investigate abuse, or respond to valid legal requests.
  • During business or operational changes: if the app or related assets are transferred, data may be transferred as part of that transition subject to applicable law.

Advertising and AI features

University Hub uses Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network to show ads. Google AdMob serves a banner on the home screen. Meta Audience Network serves an interstitial when you open a subject. Ad providers may collect device-level signals such as advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, and interaction data according to their own policies.

University Hub also offers AI-assisted study features. When you choose to use those tools, the text, files, prompts, OCR output, and related inputs required for the feature may be processed by Google AI or Gemini-related services to generate summaries, questions, explanations, or similar results.

If you do not want content processed for a specific AI feature, do not submit that content through the feature.

Privacy policies for the main third-party services used by University Hub:

  • Firebase privacy information
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Meta Privacy Policy, which covers Meta Audience Network.
  • Gemini API terms

Permissions and device access

University Hub may request access to certain device features only when relevant to the feature you use:

  • Camera: to capture study or profile images, or to scan a code or document when you start that feature.
  • Files you choose: to let you pick, upload, open, download, or share PDFs, images, and related files into the app. The app can also receive files you share to it from another app.
  • Notifications: to send reminders, updates, and topic-based alerts.
  • Alarms and restart: to deliver local reminders you set, including after the device restarts.
  • Network access: to sync content, sign you in, serve files, fetch academic resources, display ads, and run AI features.

You can manage permissions through your device settings. Some features may not work properly if access is denied.

Retention and deletion

We retain account data, profile information, uploads, community content, and related records for as long as needed to operate the app, provide requested features, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with law, and preserve legitimate backup or audit records.

  • Locally stored content such as downloads, reminders, caches, and preferences may remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app.
  • Community posts, uploads, comments, and similar content may remain visible until deleted by you, removed by administrators, or cleared according to operational needs.
  • If you want to request deletion of your account or associated data, contact admin@universityhub.co.in with enough information for us to identify your account.
  • We may retain limited information where necessary for fraud prevention, abuse prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or security logs.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect data, including managed cloud infrastructure, authentication controls, transport security, and access restrictions appropriate to the service. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

University Hub is intended for college and university students. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request and take reasonable steps.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app changes, legal requirements, or operational needs. When we make material updates, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page and may provide additional notice inside the app or on the website where appropriate.
