Who we are

GestureFlow is an Android productivity utility that lets you draw custom gestures to launch apps, run system actions, and trigger user-selected shortcuts from a floating bubble, notification entry, home-screen widget, or inside the app.

Privacy contact: admin@universityhub.co.in

What the app does

GestureFlow stores gesture shapes and the actions you assign to them. When you draw a saved gesture, the app compares the stroke against your saved gestures on-device and runs the matching action, such as opening an app, starting a timer, launching camera, toggling flashlight, opening maps/search, or placing a call when you configured a call action.

GestureFlow is designed to operate primarily on-device. The current app does not require an account, does not include advertising, and does not include analytics or cloud-sync features.

Data we access or store

Depending on the gestures and actions you create, GestureFlow may access or store the following information locally on your device:

  • Gesture drawings: the points that make up your saved gesture shapes, gesture names, and recognition settings.
  • Gesture actions: the action type and configuration you assign to each gesture, such as an app package name, timer duration, map/search query, phone number, or shortcut setting.
  • Launchable app catalog: app names, icons, and package names shown so you can choose which app a gesture should launch.
  • Contacts data: if you choose a contact-based call action, GestureFlow may read contact names and phone numbers so you can select the number for that action.
  • Call action data: phone numbers that you explicitly save in a gesture action are stored locally as part of that gesture configuration.
  • Camera and flashlight state: camera access may be used for camera-related actions or flashlight control, depending on the action you select.
  • Surface settings: preferences for bubble mode, notification mode, widget access, opacity, size, feedback, reliability settings, and temporary hidden/snoozed state.
  • Permission and setup state: whether Android permissions or special access such as overlay, notification, battery optimization, background access, or alarms and reminders are available.
  • Local app state: recent gestures, starter-pack preferences, onboarding/privacy acceptance, and other settings needed to run the app.

How data is used

  • To recognize your drawn gesture and run the action you assigned to it.
  • To show your saved gesture library, recent gestures, starter gestures, and action labels.
  • To launch apps, app-specific actions, system actions, timers, alarms, maps/search, camera, flashlight, or call actions that you configure.
  • To show GestureFlow outside the app through a floating bubble, notification entry, or home-screen widget when you enable those surfaces.
  • To keep gesture access reliable on Android devices that restrict background work, subject to Android system limits and your permissions.
  • To show in-app permission explanations, reliability health, and direct setup actions for features you choose to use.

Data sharing and transfers

GestureFlow's current implementation keeps gesture definitions, action configuration, contact selections, phone numbers saved into gestures, and app-surface settings on your device. The app does not use an account system, does not sync gesture data to our servers, does not sell personal data, and does not include ad or analytics SDKs in the current codebase.

  • No sale of personal data: we do not sell gesture data, contact selections, call action settings, or app usage configuration.
  • No routine third-party sharing: the current app flow does not transmit your gesture library or action settings to our servers.
  • Android platform services: actions such as calling, opening maps/search, launching another app, or setting alarms are handed to Android or the target app you selected.
  • Legal or safety requests: if we ever receive a valid legal request related to support or abuse handling, we would respond only as required by applicable law.

Future features

GestureFlow may add optional ads, one-time purchases, subscriptions, or remote push notifications in a future version. These features are not part of the current app version described above. If they are added, we will update this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety declarations before publishing the version that enables them.

  • Ads: if advertising is added, the app may use an advertising SDK that processes data such as advertising identifiers, device information, or ad interaction data according to the SDK provider's policy.
  • Purchases or subscriptions: if paid features are added, payments will be handled through Google Play Billing where required by Google Play policies. GestureFlow would receive purchase status needed to unlock paid features, but payment details are handled by Google Play.
  • Remote push notifications: if remote push is added, the app may use a push token or installation identifier so notifications can be delivered to your device. Local reminders and local app notifications do not require this kind of remote push service.

Permissions and device access

GestureFlow may request the following Android permissions or special access only when needed for selected features:

  • Display over other apps: used for the floating bubble and drawing the gesture surface above other apps.
  • Notifications: used for notification mode, foreground-service visibility, and persistent access controls on supported Android versions.
  • Foreground service and special use foreground service: used to keep the user-enabled gesture bubble or notification entry point available while showing a user-visible notification.
  • Contacts: used only when you choose contact-based actions so the app can show contact names and phone numbers for selection.
  • Phone: used only for call actions that you explicitly configure and trigger.
  • Camera: used for camera-related actions and flashlight control where Android requires camera access.
  • Alarms and reminders / exact alarms: used for timer, alarm, snooze, reminder, or recovery behavior when you choose features that need precise scheduling.
  • Battery optimization and background access settings: used to open Android settings pages that can improve reliability on devices that aggressively stop background apps.
  • Vibration: used for optional haptic feedback when gestures are recognized or actions run.

If you deny or revoke a permission, the related feature may stop working until you grant it again.

Retention and deletion

GestureFlow keeps gesture and action configuration locally on your device for as long as needed to provide the feature or until you change, delete, clear, or remove it.

  • You can delete saved gestures inside the app.
  • You can remove local app data by clearing GestureFlow storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Temporary surface state, recent gesture state, and permission status can change automatically as you use the app or change Android settings.

Security

GestureFlow stores its app data locally using Android app storage and relies on Android permission controls for sensitive features such as overlay, contacts, calls, camera, alarms, and notifications. No software or device environment can guarantee absolute security, and local app data remains subject to the security of your device and operating system.

Children's privacy

GestureFlow is a general productivity utility and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact us and we will review the request.
