What it does

Turn frequent phone tasks into gestures you remember.

GestureFlow is built for the small actions you repeat every day. Instead of hunting through apps, menus, and settings, you choose a gesture and connect it to the action you want.

A

Launch apps and shortcuts

Open installed apps, URLs, deep links, Android settings pages, calculator, downloads, camera, voice recorder, or any app-specific shortcut you configure.

Apps URLs Settings

Move faster with communication

WhatsApp Telegram SMS

How you access it

Choose the surface that fits the moment.

Floating bubble for the fastest path.

Keep the GestureFlow logo on screen, tap it, draw, and trigger your saved gesture from wherever you are.

Notification mode when you want less visual clutter.

Use a persistent notification entry point instead of a floating element, while still keeping gesture access close.

Home-screen widget for dependable recovery.

Use Draw and Open actions from the launcher, especially on phones that aggressively clear background apps.

Built for real Android behavior

Fast when possible. Honest when Android needs a permission.

Some actions need Android access, and GestureFlow asks at the right moment instead of overwhelming you on first launch.

Bubble

Overlay access

Needed only when you want the floating bubble or drawing over other apps. You stay in control and can stop it anytime.

Notification

Notification permission

Used for notification mode and foreground service visibility, so Android and the user both know GestureFlow is active.

Actions

Context permissions

Contacts, phone, camera, alarms, and similar access are explained when a gesture actually needs that capability.

Workflow

Create once, run from anywhere.

GestureFlow gives you starter packs to move quickly, then lets you fine-tune your own shapes, actions, reliability, bubble size, opacity, and recognition behavior.

Pick an action

Choose from system actions, app launches, social shortcuts, searches, timers, media controls, sequences, or custom intents.

Draw the gesture

Record a shape that feels natural to you, from simple letters to quick symbols tied to the task.

Test recognition

Use the Test screen to confirm the gesture is recognized before relying on the bubble, notification, or widget.

Improve reliability

Review health checks and direct setup actions for overlay, notification, widget, battery, background, and alarm recovery.

Privacy and control

You decide which powers GestureFlow gets.

GestureFlow is designed around user-triggered shortcuts. Permissions are tied to the features you choose, and gesture setup stays focused on your device experience.

1
Permissions are feature-based.

Overlay, notifications, contacts, phone, camera, and alarms are requested only when they support a chosen gesture or access mode.

2
You can stop live access.

Bubble and notification mode are user controlled, visible, and can be stopped from inside the app.

3
Clear explanations before sensitive actions.

GestureFlow explains why a permission matters before actions such as direct calling, flashlight, overlay drawing, or alarms.

4
Public policy ready.

The privacy policy is available at universityhub.co.in/gestureflow/privacy-policy.
